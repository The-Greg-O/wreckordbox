# Principles

The rules the DJ agreed the window follows.

1. **Fast and stripped down.** Text first, thin borders, no artwork or icons by default, nothing that needs heavy rendering. Upstream's performance budgets still apply.
2. **One frame, four jobs.** Library, Intake, Review and Stick are views on keys 1 to 4. The tree, the player and the status line are the same in all of them; only the working area changes.
3. **Listening and deciding happen together.** Wherever a track is judged (Energy, tags, cues, grid), its waveform, playback and the controls for the judgement are on screen at once.
4. **Keys for the frequent path, pointer for everything.** Nothing can only be done from the keyboard, and nothing frequent needs the pointer. Tags are the exception: they are clicked, because the Tag schema changes.
5. **The window teaches the deck.** Filtering uses the same fields and feel as the XDJ-AZ's Track Filter, keys are shown as the DJ mixes them, and the tags that reach the deck are marked.
6. **The DJ's intent is drawn from files, not built in.** Tag dimensions and their tags come from the Tag schema. Nothing in the window assumes four categories or particular names.
7. **Agents are quiet.** A Run in progress and Facts waiting show as counts. Review is a view the DJ enters; nothing interrupts.
8. **Never ambiguous about what is being edited.** Editing a Mutant, a Gig stick or a suggested value looks different from editing the core track, a Spare stick or a confirmed value.
9. **Writing a Stick is deliberate, and fun.** Nothing reaches a Stick until the DJ holds the button. The load itself is allowed to be ridiculous, and can be shrunk out of the way.
10. **Shaped for a tiled desktop.** Mostly maximised is the main case. Half width keeps every view usable; below that, panels give way in a fixed order (second list, tree, columns) and nothing overlaps.
11. **Follows Omarchy, except where colour means something.** Backgrounds, text, accent and fonts come from the active theme. Waveform bands, cue colours, Prep colours, phrase colours and key lights do not change with the theme.
12. **Waveforms are sharp.** They are drawn at device resolution at every interface scale and never as blocks.
